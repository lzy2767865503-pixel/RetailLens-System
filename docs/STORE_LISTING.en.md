## Short description

## Description

Retail Decision Studio by LAI ZEYU turns a structured retail business profile into a reproducible decision workpaper in Chinese or English. It is built from the open-source RetailLens project maintained in the linked source repository.

Designed and authored by **LAI ZEYU（来泽宇）**.

Complete a nine-step intake covering the customer, retail format, geography, channels, location, compliance, supply chain, unit economics, organization, risk, and evidence. Retail Decision Studio by LAI ZEYU then applies a versioned 100-point rubric and non-compensatory hard gates to produce:

- a deterministic score that is identical for identical inputs;
- strengths, gaps, evidence quality, confidence, and prioritized actions;
- Five Forces, competitive profile, STP and positioning, entry-mode, productivity, service-gap, organization/control, and risk analysis;
- EFE, IFE, IE, and QSPM strategy views; and
- a management-ready 30/60/90-day decision workpaper.

The core assessment works offline after installation and does not require an account or API key. Optional OpenAI interpretation uses your own API key only after an explicit request. AI receives locked results as read-only context and cannot change the score. OpenAI is a separate third-party service: this product does not include an OpenAI API key, API credits, or a ChatGPT subscription, and OpenAI API usage may incur separate charges under your OpenAI account.

Your draft stays in local application storage. Retail Decision Studio by LAI ZEYU has no advertising, analytics, or developer-operated telemetry. Use About & privacy to clear locally saved business data.

The included demo is synthetic and is not a claim about a real company or current market conditions. Retail Decision Studio by LAI ZEYU is decision support, not legal, tax, accounting, investment, or regulatory advice.
